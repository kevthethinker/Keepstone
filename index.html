Where it earns its keep

Four industries, two examples each — the kind of gap between two dates, two documents, or two rules that's easy to miss until it costs you.

Local Government

What's the required public notice period before a zoning variance hearing?

The ordinance requires at least 15 days' published notice before a zoning variance hearing, plus mailed notice to property owners within 250 feet of the parcel.

Source: Municipal Code, Chapter 12, "Zoning Variance Procedures" — verify anytime by opening the original document.

When is our final report due for the Community Development Block Grant from last year?

The grant agreement requires a final expenditure report within 90 days of project completion. Completion was logged as June 30, 2026, making the report due by September 28, 2026.

Source: CDBG Grant Agreement, Section 9, "Reporting Requirements"; Project Completion Log — verify anytime by opening the original documents.

Manufacturing

If we switch to the new sealant on the gasket line, does that affect our warranty terms with any customers?

Yes — the standard customer warranty requires sealants rated for continuous use above 200°F. The proposed sealant's data sheet lists only a 180°F rating, which would void the warranty as written.

Source: Customer Warranty Terms, Section 4.1; Sealant X Technical Data Sheet, "Temperature Rating" — verify anytime by opening the original documents.

Is the torque spec in the 2019 assembly manual still correct for the new bracket?

No. The 2019 manual lists 35 ft-lb, but Engineering Change Notice EC-114 (2024) raised it to 42 ft-lb for the redesigned bracket. Use the updated spec.

Source: Engineering Change Notice EC-114, "Bracket Torque Revision" — supersedes Assembly Manual 2019, Section 6.2.

Legal

What's the termination notice period in our Ashworth Group service agreement?

Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice, or immediately for uncured material breach after a 15-day cure period.

Source: Ashworth Group Master Service Agreement, Section 12, "Termination" — verify anytime by opening the original document.

The Henderson contract renews every March 15 — what's our deadline to send a termination-for-convenience notice?

The Henderson Master Agreement requires 90 days' written notice before the renewal date, so notice must be sent no later than December 15 to avoid automatic renewal for another year.

Source: Henderson Master Agreement, Section 8, "Term and Renewal" — verify anytime by opening the original document.

Healthcare Administration

When is Jane Doe's next care plan review due?

Jane Doe's last care plan review was completed on June 4, 2026. State regulations require a review at least every 90 days, so the next one is due no later than September 2, 2026.

Source: Resident Care Plan Log — Jane Doe; State Long-Term Care Compliance Manual, Section 7, "Care Plan Review Intervals" — verify anytime by opening the original documents.

How many days do we have to submit a claim to Larkspur Health Plan after the date of service?

Larkspur Health Plan requires claims to be submitted within 90 days of the date of service; claims submitted after that window are denied regardless of medical necessity.

Source: Payer Reference Guide 2026, "Larkspur Health Plan — Timely Filing Requirements" — verify anytime by opening the original document.
